\takeawaybox{Addressing Water Losses \& Manual Billing Bottlenecks in Rwanda}

\textbf{Context:} In Rwanda, municipal utility operators (WASAC) and citizens face severe water management challenges. Non-revenue water losses exceed 38\%, caused by undetected pipe leaks and unmonitored communal taps.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Affected Users:} WASAC utility staff struggling with delayed manual reading cycles, and residential households suffering from unexpected high bills.
  \item \textbf{Existing Bottlenecks:} Manual billing cycles delay leak detection by weeks, leading to massive resource waste and billing disputes.
  \item \textbf{Target Solution:} AquaTrack introduces automated IoT telemetry ingestion, real-time leak alerts, and transparent citizen/WASAC web dashboards.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Recommended pattern:} In \underline{Rwanda's water sector}, \underline{WASAC staff and citizens} struggle with \underline{undetected leaks and manual billing delays}. Existing approaches fall short because \underline{they rely on periodic manual meter reads}. We designed AquaTrack to improve \underline{real-time visibility and leak response}.
