\textbf{Database --- PostgreSQL + Sequelize ORM}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Chosen for its relational model: households, meters, and readings have clear foreign-key relationships a document store cannot enforce natively.
  \item Sequelize hooks handle bcrypt password hashing on \texttt{beforeCreate}; \texttt{sync(\{alter:true\})} allows rapid schema iteration during development.
  \item \textit{Trade-off:} \texttt{alter:true} is unsafe in production --- a migration-based approach is the planned next step.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}
\textbf{Meter Simulator --- FastAPI (Python, deployed on Railway)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Three vendor types: \textbf{Kamstrup} (10 residential meters), \textbf{Susteq} (5 communal taps), \textbf{EoI} (5 prepaid home meters).
  \item Readings accumulate by elapsed time $\times$ time-of-day usage curve --- near-zero flow at night, peak at 06:00--09:00 and 17:00--21:00.
  \item Alarms are condition-triggered and persist across polls, not re-rolled randomly.
  \item \textit{Trade-off:} State is in-memory --- a restart resets cumulative readings.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}
\textbf{Authentication --- Stateless JWT}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Two token keys enforce role separation: \texttt{aquatrack\_token} (WASAC staff) and \texttt{citizen\_token} (citizens).
  \item \textit{Trade-off:} Tokens cannot be invalidated before expiry without a blacklist --- acceptable for project scope.
\end{itemize}
